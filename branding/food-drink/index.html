What your food & drink brand identity includes

Food brands live on cups, bags, menus, signage and delivery apps. I design bold, readable logos, a colour palette with personality, menu and social templates, and packaging mockups, so the brand looks right on a sticker, a shopfront and a phone screen.

Every package includes vector source files and the copyright, because every concept is designed by me rather than generated by AI. Your first full brand board arrives within 48 hours of the brief; Basic and Premium are completed in 2 days, The Brand Ecosystem in 5 business days.

How the process works

  • Brief: a short call about your business, customers and the look you want.
  • Research and concepts: I study your market and competitors, then design logo concepts, colours, type and mockups.
  • Refine and launch: we revise until it feels right, then you receive every file and the guidelines.

Questions about food & drink branding

Can you design menus, cups and takeaway packaging?

Yes. Packaging and merchandising are part of The Brand Ecosystem package, and every package includes mockups so you can see your brand on cups, bags and signage before printing anything.

I'm opening a new café. When should I start the branding?

Ideally before you order signage, cups or printed menus, so everything is produced once in the final design. The first brand board takes 48 hours, so branding rarely holds up an opening.

Do you design for packaged food and drink products?

Yes. The portfolio includes pet food, a brewery, a matcha drink brand and snack brands. Product packaging is included in The Brand Ecosystem or available as a custom quote.

How much does restaurant or café branding cost?

Packages start at $250. Most new cafés and restaurants choose Premium Branding ($450) for three logo directions and a social kit; The Brand Ecosystem ($1,050) adds packaging and merchandise.

Can you design a mascot or pattern for my food brand?

Yes. Patterns and graphic elements are part of the identity system, and The Brand Ecosystem includes pattern and icon assets.
