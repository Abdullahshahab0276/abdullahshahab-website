Questions about food & drink branding

Can you design menus, cups and takeaway packaging?

Yes. Packaging and merchandising are part of The Brand Ecosystem package, and every package includes mockups so you can see your brand on cups, bags and signage before printing anything.

I'm opening a new café. When should I start the branding?

Ideally before you order signage, cups or printed menus, so everything is produced once in the final design. The first brand board takes 48 hours, so branding rarely holds up an opening.

Do you design for packaged food and drink products?

Yes. The portfolio includes pet food, a brewery, a matcha drink brand and snack brands. Product packaging is included in The Brand Ecosystem or available as a custom quote.
