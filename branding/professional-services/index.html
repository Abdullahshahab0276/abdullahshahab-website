Questions about professional services branding

Do law firms have branding rules?

Bar associations have advertising rules about claims and wording, not about visual design. Check marketing copy with your bar's rules; the identity itself is flexible.

Can you design proposal and slide templates?

Yes. Document and presentation templates are part of The Brand Ecosystem package.

How much does a law firm logo cost?

Packages start at $250. Firms that need proposal, letterhead and slide templates usually choose The Brand Ecosystem ($1,050).
