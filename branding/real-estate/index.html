Questions about real estate branding

Can I use my own logo with my brokerage?

Usually yes, but brokerages and state real estate commissions have advertising rules about showing the brokerage name. Check with your broker before printing.

Do you design yard signs and riders?

Yes. Sign and rider designs are included as mockups in every package and designed as print files in The Brand Ecosystem.

How much does a realtor logo cost?

Packages start at $250. Most agents and teams choose Premium Branding ($450) for three concepts, guidelines and a social media kit.
