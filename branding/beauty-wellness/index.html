Questions about beauty & wellness branding

Can you design packaging for my skincare or beauty range?

Yes. Packaging and merchandising are included in The Brand Ecosystem package, and the Basic and Premium packages include 3D mockups of your brand on real products. For a large range, I'll put together a custom quote.

Do you work with spas, salons and wellness studios too?

Yes. The portfolio includes a med spa, a pilates studio, a mental wellbeing app and wellness lifestyle brands. The process is the same: a short brief call, research into your market, then your first full brand board in 48 hours.

Will my brand work on Instagram as well as on packaging?

That's the goal. From the Premium Branding package up you get a social media kit with profile assets and post templates in your new identity.
