What your beauty & wellness brand identity includes

For beauty and wellness brands the identity has to work on small labels, frosted bottles and Instagram grids at once. I design the logo system, a restrained colour palette, elegant typography and packaging-ready layouts, then test them on 3D product mockups so you see the brand on the shelf before you print.

Every package includes vector source files and the copyright, because every concept is designed by me rather than generated by AI. Your first full brand board arrives within 48 hours of the brief; Basic and Premium are completed in 2 days, The Brand Ecosystem in 5 business days.

How the process works

  • Brief: a short call about your business, customers and the look you want.
  • Research and concepts: I study your market and competitors, then design logo concepts, colours, type and mockups.
  • Refine and launch: we revise until it feels right, then you receive every file and the guidelines.

Questions about beauty & wellness branding

Can you design packaging for my skincare or beauty range?

Yes. Packaging and merchandising are included in The Brand Ecosystem package, and the Basic and Premium packages include 3D mockups of your brand on real products. For a large range, I'll put together a custom quote.

Do you work with spas, salons and wellness studios too?

Yes. The portfolio includes a med spa, a pilates studio, a mental wellbeing app and wellness lifestyle brands. The process is the same: a short brief call, research into your market, then your first full brand board in 48 hours.

Will my brand work on Instagram as well as on packaging?

That's the goal. From the Premium Branding package up you get a social media kit with profile assets and post templates in your new identity.

How much does skincare branding cost?

Packages start at $250 for a logo with colours, type, mockups and guidelines. Most skincare founders choose Premium Branding ($450) for three directions and a social kit, or The Brand Ecosystem ($1,050) when packaging is needed.

Can you follow cosmetic labelling rules?

I design the label layout and leave space for the required information. The exact wording and claims should be checked against the rules in your market; my skincare guide covers the US and UK basics.
