What your fashion & luxury brand identity includes

Luxury depends on restraint and detail. I design monograms and wordmarks, refined typography, a tight palette, and applications on tags, boxes, bags and campaign visuals, so the brand feels expensive in every small detail.

Every package includes vector source files and the copyright, because every concept is designed by me rather than generated by AI. Your first full brand board arrives within 48 hours of the brief; Basic and Premium are completed in 2 days, The Brand Ecosystem in 5 business days.

How the process works

  • Brief: a short call about your business, customers and the look you want.
  • Research and concepts: I study your market and competitors, then design logo concepts, colours, type and mockups.
  • Refine and launch: we revise until it feels right, then you receive every file and the guidelines.
