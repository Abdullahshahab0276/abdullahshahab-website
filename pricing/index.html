Which branding package should I choose?

  • Basic plan, $250: for a new business that needs one strong logo and the essentials to launch.
  • Premium Branding, $450: the most popular choice. Three directions to compare, plus complete guidelines and a social media kit to roll the brand out on day one.
  • The Brand Ecosystem, $1,050: for brands ready to market and advertise. Adds strategy, brand voice, social and ad creatives, packaging and UI assets.

How do these prices compare with the market?

Branding prices vary widely. A UK benchmark puts a freelance logo at about £500 to £1,000 and a logo with guidelines at £1,500 to £5,000 (Whito, 2026). Marketplace studio packs such as 99designs' Full-Service Brand Pack cost £3,499 (99designs).

My prices are lower because there is no agency overhead: no offices, account managers or handoffs. The full breakdown is in how much does a logo cost in 2026.

Packages & pricing FAQs

Are these one-time prices?

Yes. Prices are in US dollars and paid once. There is no subscription or retainer.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes. You can start with the Basic plan and add more assets later. Book a call and we'll scope the next step.

Can I set up a payment plan?

We agree the payment structure before any work begins. Book a call with your scope and we'll find an arrangement that fits.

What if I need something outside the packages?

Packaging ranges, extra social creatives or other one-off work can be quoted separately. Send a message for a custom quote.

Do you sign NDAs?

Yes. Scope is confirmed in writing, and I'm happy to work under an NDA or formal agreement if your business needs one.

Are prices the same for US and UK clients?

Yes. Prices are fixed in US dollars for every client, wherever you are.
