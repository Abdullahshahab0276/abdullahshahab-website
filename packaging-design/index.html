What packaging do you design?

  • Labels for bottles, jars, tubes and cans
  • Boxes, mailers and unboxing inserts
  • Pouches and bags for food, coffee and pet products
  • Merchandise such as tote bags, t-shirts, cups and stickers
  • Product mockups for your website, marketplace listings and ads

Why design packaging and brand together?

Packaging designed after the fact often fights the logo: new colours, new fonts, a different mood. When the identity and the packaging are designed as one system, every product looks like it belongs to the same brand, and adding new flavours or SKUs is simple.

See packaging work in the portfolio, such as the MAWS cat food pouches, PEPNOW serum bottles, Velora haircare and Solva supplements, and read the skincare brand identity guide for what premium beauty packaging gets right.

Packaging design FAQs

Do you provide print-ready packaging files?

Yes. Final artwork is delivered as print-ready files with your source files, ready to send to your printer or manufacturer.

Can you work with my printer's dieline?

Yes. Send the dieline or template from your printer or manufacturer and I'll design to it.

Can you add required label information?

I design the layout and leave space for required information such as ingredients and warnings. The exact wording must be checked against the rules in your market.

Can I order packaging design without a full rebrand?

Yes, as a custom project, as long as your existing logo and brand files are available.
