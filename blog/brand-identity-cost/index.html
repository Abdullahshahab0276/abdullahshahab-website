How Much Does a Brand Identity Cost in 2026? US and UK Prices

A brand identity costs from about $300 to $15,000+ in the US and £1,500 to £20,000 in the UK. Real 2026 prices by provider, and what each level includes.

Abdullah ShahabBrand designer · November 20, 2026 · 5 min read

A complete brand identity costs anywhere from about $300 to over $15,000 in the US, and roughly £1,500 to £20,000 in the UK, depending on who designs it and how much strategy is included. Most small businesses and startups land between $500 and $5,000. The price depends less on the logo and more on everything around it: guidelines, applications, strategy and how many people work on it.

This guide covers brand identities, not just logos. For logo-only prices, read how much does a logo cost in 2026.

What counts as a brand identity?

A brand identity is the full visual system: logo and its variations, colour palette, typography, imagery style, brand guidelines and the first set of applications such as stationery, social templates and packaging. The brand identity checklist lists every deliverable to ask for.

How much does a brand identity cost in the US?

Provider Typical 2026 price What’s usually included
Design contest (logo and brand guide) $329–$1,199 Many concepts from different designers, one winner, a brand guide
Freelancer on a marketplace From about $1,000–$4,000 for an identity package One designer, logo, palette, type, guidelines
Independent studio Often $2,500–$6,500 Discovery, identity system, guidelines, some applications
Branding agency $5,000 to $50,000+ Research, strategy, full system, rollout, often a website

Contest prices come from 99designs’ Logo & brand guide packages, which run from $329 (Bronze) to $1,199 (Platinum). On Dribbble’s services marketplace, startup identity packages are listed from about $1,411 to $4,000. An agency package on Clutch shows three tiers from $2,300 to $6,500. Shopify puts mid-range branding packages with strategy at $5,000 to $50,000.

How much does a brand identity cost in the UK?

A 2026 UK benchmark from Whito gives these levels:

Level UK price (2026)
Professional logo (freelancer to small agency) £500–£3,000
Brand starter pack (logo + guidelines) £1,500–£5,000
Full brand identity £5,000–£20,000
Complete rebrand with strategy £10,000–£50,000+

For comparison, the 99designs Full-Service Brand Pack is listed at £3,499. A US/UK pricing guide from Graphic Design Junction puts a complete branding system at $3,000–$15,000+ in the US and £2,500–£12,000+ in the UK.

Why do brand identity prices vary so much?

  • Strategy. Workshops, research and positioning documents add the most cost. A visual identity without them is far cheaper.
  • Team size. An agency bills for strategists, account managers and several designers. One designer doesn’t.
  • Number of applications. Ten packaging designs cost more than one business card.
  • Location and overhead. Office costs in London or New York are built into the price, not into the design.
  • Rounds and timeline. Six weeks of meetings costs more than a focused 48-hour first draft.

How much should a startup spend on its brand identity?

Match the budget to the stage of the business:

  • Pre-launch or testing an idea: a logo with basic guidelines. Budget $250–$1,000.
  • Launching to customers: a complete identity with three directions, guidelines and a social kit. Budget $450–$3,000.
  • Raising money or entering a crowded market: add strategy, voice and marketing assets. Budget $1,000 to agency level.

Spending more doesn’t guarantee a better result. Ask for the designer’s own past work, the exact list of files, how many concepts and revisions you get, and who owns the copyright.

The bottom line

In the US, expect $300 to $1,200 from a contest, $1,000 to $4,000 from an experienced freelancer, and $5,000 or more from an agency. In the UK, a starter identity is £1,500 to £5,000 and a full agency identity is £5,000 to £20,000. Decide what you need first (logo, system or strategy), then pay for that level and no more.
