How can a logo be done in 48 hours?

Speed comes from process, not shortcuts. One designer (me) handles the whole project, so there are no handovers or account managers. The brief call collects everything up front, and I work while you sleep: a brief sent at the end of a US or UK day is usually designed overnight.

What you still get, even when it's fast

  • Market and competitor research before any sketching
  • Up to 3 original concepts (Premium)
  • AI, EPS, SVG, PDF and PNG source files
  • Written copyright transfer

When you shouldn't rush

If you haven't settled on a name, or you're unsure who your customers are, take a day to decide first. A fast logo for the wrong name costs more than a slow one. My guide to brand identity basics helps you prepare.

48-hour logo design FAQs

Can you deliver a logo in 24 hours?

The first brand board is promised within 48 hours. If you have a hard deadline, mention it on the brief call and I'll tell you honestly whether it's possible.

Is a fast logo lower quality?

No. The time saved comes from one designer and no meetings, not from skipping research or using templates.

Do you charge extra for rush delivery?

No. 48-hour first concepts are standard on every package.
