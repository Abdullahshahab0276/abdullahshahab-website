Bilingual branding for Florida businesses

About 30.6% of Floridians aged 5 and over speak a language other than English at home (Census, 2020–2024), and Spanish is by far the most common, followed by Haitian Creole and Portuguese. In cities like Hialeah and Doral the share is near 90%. I design identities that work for both audiences.

What bilingual branding means in practice

  • Names that are easy to say and spell in English and Spanish, and checked for unwanted meanings
  • Taglines written as pairs, not word-for-word translations
  • Fonts with full accent support (á, é, ñ, ü) at every weight
  • Guidelines that show how to set both languages on signage, menus and social posts

FAQs

Do you speak Spanish?

I design in both languages and check copy with native speakers or with your team. For long marketing copy, I recommend a native copywriter.

Should my Florida logo include Spanish text?

Usually not. Keep the logo itself language-neutral and put bilingual taglines and messaging around it, so the mark works everywhere.

How much does a logo cost in Florida?

My prices are the same everywhere in Florida: $250 for a logo with colours, type, mockups and guidelines; $450 for three concepts, complete guidelines and a social kit; $1,050 for The Brand Ecosystem. Agency projects in Miami or Tampa commonly cost several thousand dollars for similar deliverables.

Do I need to register my business name before designing a logo in Florida?

If you trade under any name other than your own legal name or your LLC or corporation's registered name, Florida requires a fictitious name (DBA) registration with the Division of Corporations on Sunbiz. The fee is $50. It's worth checking Sunbiz and the trademark records before finalising a name, so the logo is built on a name you can keep.
