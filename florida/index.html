Florida branding FAQs

Who designs logos for Florida small businesses?

I'm Abdullah Shahab, an independent brand identity designer with 7 years of experience. I work remotely with Florida founders and small businesses in every county: every logo is designed by hand (no AI generators), priced from $250, with source files and full copyright included.

How much does a logo cost in Florida?

My prices are the same everywhere in Florida: $250 for a logo with colours, type, mockups and guidelines; $450 for three concepts, complete guidelines and a social kit; $1,050 for The Brand Ecosystem. Agency projects in Miami or Tampa commonly cost several thousand dollars for similar deliverables.

Do I need to register my business name before designing a logo in Florida?

If you trade under any name other than your own legal name or your LLC or corporation's registered name, Florida requires a fictitious name (DBA) registration with the Division of Corporations on Sunbiz. The fee is $50. It's worth checking Sunbiz and the trademark records before finalising a name, so the logo is built on a name you can keep.

Can I trademark my logo in Florida?

Yes. Florida registers trademarks and service marks with the Division of Corporations for $87.50 per class, and a registration lasts 5 years. A state registration covers Florida only; a federal USPTO registration covers the whole country. I don't file trademarks, but every logo I design is original and comes with clean files for the application.

Can you design a bilingual English and Spanish brand?

Yes. About 30.6% of Floridians speak a language other than English at home, mostly Spanish, so many Florida brands need a name, tagline and type system that work in both languages. I design type systems with full Spanish character support and test taglines in both languages.

Do you meet Florida clients in person?

No. I work remotely from Pakistan, and that's what keeps prices fixed and turnaround fast. Calls are booked on Calendly in Eastern or Central Time, and a brief sent at the end of your day is worked on overnight.
