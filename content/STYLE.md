# Writing rules for the Shahab blog

## Voice and positioning
- First person, written as Shahab, a brand designer. Plain, confident, helpful. No hype, no fluff.
- Positioning: human-designed branding, "designed by a person, not a prompt". Never describe the
  service as AI-powered. Do not claim that AI is never used in reels or video work.
- Audience: founders and small-business owners in the USA, UK and other premium markets.
  Use US spelling in titles; either is fine in body text but keep one article consistent.

## Facts you may use (do not invent others)
- Packages: Basic $250 (1 concept, 2 revisions, 2-day delivery); Premium Branding $450 (3 concepts,
  full guidelines, social kit, 3 revisions, 2-day delivery); The Brand Ecosystem $1,050 (strategy,
  marketing assets, 5 business days, unlimited revisions on the chosen concept). Custom quotes available.
- First full brand board in 48 hours. Source files and copyright included. Scope confirmed in writing;
  NDAs on request. Shahab does not file trademarks but prepares files for an attorney.
- Portfolio brands (in index.html PROJECTS list and img/): Voitex, Ovenox, Mavion, Pingup, Metmel,
  Alovate, Oxtero, Marrow, FileQ, GROWIFY, Nepay, Féréa, and others. Describe only what the images show.
- Do NOT invent client names, quotes, testimonials, statistics about Shahab's business, results,
  client counts, or years of experience. Only 3 client reviews exist (Omar, Jennifer Miller,
  Michael Anderson) and their wording is in index.html.

## Research and accuracy
- Search the web for every price range, statistic, law or platform fact. Cite 2–4 credible sources
  in a "Sources:" line at the end with links. Paraphrase; never copy sentences from sources.
- Legal or financial topics end with a one-line "not legal/financial advice" note.

## Structure and SEO
- 1,200–1,800 words. One clear search intent per article; the title contains the main keyword.
- Front matter: title (≤ 65 chars where possible), slug (short, hyphenated, keyword-first),
  description (140–160 chars), date (publish day, YYYY-MM-DD), category, cover (an existing image in
  img/ that fits the topic; prefer ones not yet used as a cover), cover_alt (describe the image).
- Open with the answer in the first 3 paragraphs. Use `##` headings that read like questions people
  search. Include at least one bullet list or table. Add a short "How I handle this for clients" or
  similar section tying back to the packages, without being salesy.
- Link to 1–2 earlier articles (../<slug>/index.html) and to ../../index.html#pricing or #work.
- No placeholder text, no "[insert …]", no fake data.

## After publishing
- Write repurposing copy and save it to content/social/<slug>.md:
  3 Instagram captions (hook + value + CTA to the article, 5–8 hashtags), 1 LinkedIn post,
  2 Pinterest pin titles + descriptions.
