# Content plan: abdullahshahab.com

Goal: win organic clients (founders in the USA, UK and other premium markets) through Google,
with every article also repurposed for Instagram, LinkedIn and Pinterest.

## Cadence
- Publish ONE article every day (scheduled task, 2:40 PM Asia/Karachi).
- Every 3rd article is a Florida-local article (marked FL below), because the site is built to rank in
  every Florida city, town and county (/florida/, /florida/<city>/, /florida/<county>-county/).

## How topics are chosen (real search data)
Topics come from keywords people actually search. Volumes = US monthly Google searches, KD = keyword
difficulty 0-100 (OpenSEO / DataForSEO, pulled 2026-11-22). Prefer high volume + low KD. Each article
targets ONE main keyword (put it in the title, H1, first paragraph, slug and description) and answers
the related questions people ask (check Google "People also ask" and autocomplete while researching).

## Local linking rule (rank in every city and town)
- Every article links to /florida/ or a relevant Florida page at least once, where it fits naturally.
- Industry articles (restaurant, cleaning, realtor, salon, gym, construction...) end with a short
  "Working with a business in Florida?" line linking 3-5 relevant Florida city pages
  (e.g. /florida/miami/, /florida/tampa/, /florida/orlando/, /florida/jacksonville/) and the matching
  Florida service page (e.g. /florida/restaurant-branding/, /florida/real-estate-branding/).
- FL articles must link 8-15 Florida city or county pages that are relevant to the topic (all exist:
  see content/us_cities.json for slugs with state "FL", and /florida/<county>-county/ for counties),
  plus /florida/zip-codes/ where ZIPs come up. Use real Florida facts with sources (Sunbiz, FL statutes,
  Census, county sites). Never invent local details.
