## LinkedIn post
Most people see your restaurant's logo on a phone before they ever see your sign.

In a December 2024 Chatmeter survey of 1,083 US adults, 46% said they wouldn't eat at a restaurant without reading reviews first, and 36% said the menu is the first thing they check. Your logo sits right next to both: on Google, on delivery apps, on your menu.

So a restaurant logo has to:

• Read clearly at thumbnail size (Google recommends a 720 x 720 px image, minimum 250 x 250)
• Signal your price point and mood before anyone sees a dish
• Come in a full version, a short version and a square icon
• Work in one color for cups, boxes and bags
• Be supplied as vector files for signs and wraps

And no, red and yellow don't "make people hungry". The evidence is mixed. They grab attention, which is why fast food uses them.
