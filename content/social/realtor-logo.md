## LinkedIn post
Most realtor logo guides skip the part that gets agents into trouble: the rules.

A few that surprise people:

• NAR says the REALTOR® block R logo goes next to your firm logo, never inside it. No merging, framing, tilting or using the R as the first letter of "Realty".
• REALTOR can't be part of your business name. "Blackacre, Inc., REALTORS®" is fine. "Blackacre REALTORS®, Inc." isn't.
• In Florida, a team name can't include words like Realty, Brokerage, Properties or LLC, and it can't be printed larger than the brokerage name (Rule 61J2-10.026).

So a team logo needs a lockup with room for the brokerage name, designed in from day one, not squeezed in later.
