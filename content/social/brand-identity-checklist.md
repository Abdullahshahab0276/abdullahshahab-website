## LinkedIn post
"Logo design" on a quote can mean one PNG file. A brand identity means a system.

Here's the checklist I'd give any founder before they hire a designer:

• Logo system: primary, horizontal, stacked, an icon for favicons and profile pictures, and one-color versions
• Color palette with HEX and RGB for screens, CMYK for print, Pantone for packaging
• Typography: headline and body fonts, plus which ones need a commercial license
• File package: SVG, AI or EPS, PDF, PNG and JPG, in color, black and white
• Brand guidelines: clear space, minimum size, misuse examples, color and type specs
• Applications: business cards, social kit, mockups

Why bother? Small details break first. Instagram shows your profile picture at 110 x 110 pixels in a circle. A long wordmark squeezed in there turns into a blur, so you need a proper icon.

And consistency pays: in a Lucidpress survey of 200+ organizations, companies with consistently presented brands reported revenue gains of up to 33%.
