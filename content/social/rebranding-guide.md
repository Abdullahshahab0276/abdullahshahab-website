## LinkedIn post
Most failed rebrands don't fail because of the new logo. They fail because customers lost the thing they recognized, and nobody told them why.

Two examples:

• Tropicana, 2009: unit sales of Pure Premium fell 20% between January 1 and February 22 after a carton redesign (IRI data via Ad Age). The old pack was back in under two months.
• Cracker Barrel, 2025: the chain removed the "Old Timer" from its logo, lost nearly $100 million in market value (CBS News), and brought him back within days.

If you're planning a rebrand:
1. Write down why, and record your current numbers.
2. Decide what stays: a color, a symbol, the name.
3. Tell customers before they notice.
4. Switch every touchpoint in one short window, including 301 redirects and your Google Business Profile.
