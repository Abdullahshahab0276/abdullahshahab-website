## Instagram caption 3 (reel hook)
Your skincare label isn't just design. It's regulation too 🧴

US (FDA): product identity, net quantity, ingredients, business name and a US contact for adverse event reports.
UK: responsible person's name and address, ingredients, batch number, and a best-before date or the open-jar symbol.

## LinkedIn post
People can't test your serum through a screen. So they judge it by how it looks.

McKinsey's 2026 State of Beauty report puts e-commerce at 28% of global beauty sales, and expects TikTok alone to reach about $4 billion in US beauty sales this year. Your product is often seen first as a thumbnail.

Here's what premium skincare brands get right:

• A simple mark plus a monogram that works on a cap, a sachet and a profile picture
• A narrow palette: soft neutrals, one or two brand colors, one accent used with restraint
• Two typefaces, with a body font that stays readable on a 6-point ingredient list
• One packaging system, so every new SKU still looks like part of the family
• Labels planned early. In the US that now includes a contact for adverse event reports (required since December 2024). In the UK it includes the responsible person, batch number and a best-before date or open-jar symbol.

None of this needs a luxury budget. It needs decisions made before the first print run.
