## LinkedIn post
Florida's cottage food law is one of the most generous in the US, and most home bakers don't use it to its full potential.

What it allows:
• Up to $250,000 in gross sales a year
• No FDACS license, no routine inspection
• Sales in person, online or by mail, including USPS and commercial carriers
• Local governments can't ban a cottage food operation (the law preempts them)

What it doesn't:
• Wholesale, consignment or sales through cafés and shops
• Foods that need refrigeration, like cream cheese frosting, pumpkin pie or salsa

The part that matters for branding: every product needs a label with your name and address, ingredients by weight, net weight, allergens and one required statement in 10-point type. That's a design problem, not just a legal one. Plan a front panel for the brand and a fixed back panel for the rules, and every product looks consistent.
