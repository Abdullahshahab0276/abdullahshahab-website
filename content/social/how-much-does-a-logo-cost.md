## LinkedIn post
How much does a logo cost in 2026?

I looked at current pricing across the market so founders can compare like for like:

• AI logo makers and templates: $0–$200
• Fiverr: under $55 for value gigs, $125+ for high-end (Fiverr's own Aug 2026 guide)
• Upwork: $15–$30/hr median, top designers mostly $35–$50/hr
• 99designs contests: $249 (Bronze) to $1,050 (Platinum)
• Senior freelancers: $1,500 and up
• Branding agencies: $2,500 to $10,000+

The useful question isn't "what's the cheapest logo?" It's "what does this logo need to do, and what does that take?"

When you compare quotes, check five things: number of concepts, revision rounds, vector source files, brand guidelines, and copyright transfer in writing.
