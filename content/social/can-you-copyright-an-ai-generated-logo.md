## LinkedIn post
Can you copyright an AI-generated logo?

Short answer for founders in 2026: in the US, no, not if it was generated entirely by AI.

• The US Copyright Office (Jan 2025): human authorship is required; prompts alone aren't enough.
• Thaler v. Perlmutter: the Supreme Court declined review on March 2, 2026, so the human-authorship rule stands.
• UK: the government's March 2026 report recommends removing protection for AI-only works.

Why it matters more for a logo than almost anything else: it's on every asset you own, it's what copycats imitate, and investors will ask who owns it.

You can still trademark a mark through use, but the strongest position is both: original artwork you own outright, plus a registered trademark.
