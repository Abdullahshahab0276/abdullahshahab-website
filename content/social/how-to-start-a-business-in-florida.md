## LinkedIn post
The most expensive misunderstanding I see from new Florida founders:

"I registered the name on Sunbiz, so it's mine."

It isn't. Florida's own fictitious name FAQ says registration is for public notice only. It doesn't give you ownership of the name or stop anyone else using it. An LLC name only stops another Florida entity filing with the same name.

Real protection comes from a trademark:
• Florida trademark: about $87.50 per class, Florida only
• Federal (USPTO): $350 per class base fee, nationwide

A few other Florida numbers worth knowing:
• LLC formation: $125 on Sunbiz
• DBA (fictitious name): $50, valid 5 years, plus a newspaper notice
• LLC annual report: $138.75, or $538.75 if received after May 1
• No statewide business license, but most businesses need a local business tax receipt from their county and/or city

Search the name before you pay for a logo, a sign or a van wrap. Ten minutes now can save a full rebrand later.
