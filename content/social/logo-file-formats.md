Still sending EPS logo files? Here's why that's a problem 👀

Use SVG for screens and PDF for print instead. Keep one EPS only for the supplier who asks.

## LinkedIn post
Most founders get a folder of logo files and no explanation of which one to use.

Here's the cheat sheet I give clients:

• SVG for your website, app and Microsoft 365 documents. It stays sharp at any size.
• PNG for social media, slides, Canva and email signatures. Transparent background, several sizes.
• PDF for printers, agencies and brand guidelines. Vector, and anyone can open it.
• AI (or another editable vector source) as your master file. This is what any future designer will ask for.
• EPS only when a sign maker or older print system requests it. Microsoft turned off EPS in Office back in 2017 for security reasons, and in Microsoft 365 you can't insert it at all.
• JPG for photos and previews, never as your logo.

You also need the right color versions: full color, reversed for dark backgrounds, one-color black, plus RGB for screens and CMYK for print.

One rule to remember: design in vector, export to raster. If all you have is a PNG, you have a picture of your logo, not the logo itself.
