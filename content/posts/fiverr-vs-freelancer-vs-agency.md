**Short answer:** use Fiverr if your budget is under about $150 and you can judge design quality yourself. Hire an independent freelance designer if you want a complete, original identity for roughly $300 to $2,000. Hire an agency when you also need strategy, naming, campaigns and a team, usually at $3,000 to $10,000 or more.

## Prices at a glance

| Option | Typical logo price | Typical brand identity price | Timeline |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |

| Independent designer | $300 to $2,000 | $450 to $2,500 | 2 days to 3 weeks |

## Fiverr: fast and cheap, quality varies

Fiverr is a marketplace with thousands of logo sellers at every price. The best sellers are excellent; many low-priced gigs reuse templates or stock icons.

**Good for:** a quick logo on a tight budget, small side projects, simple text logos.
**Watch for:** templates sold to several buyers, unclear file ownership, "unlimited revisions" that mean small tweaks of one idea, and no research into your market.
**Tip:** ask for vector source files and a written copyright transfer before you pay.

## Independent freelance designer: one person, the whole system

An independent designer works with you directly, usually from their own site or Dribbble, Behance or Contra. You get the same person from brief to final files.

**Good for:** founders who want an original logo plus colours, type, guidelines and mockups, without agency overheads.
**Watch for:** check the portfolio is really theirs (look for full case studies, not just single logos) and that the price includes source files.

## Branding agency: strategy and a team

An agency brings strategists, designers, copywriters and project managers. You pay for the process as much as the design.

**Good for:** funded companies, rebrands of established businesses, and projects that need research, naming, messaging and a launch campaign.
**Watch for:** juniors doing the actual design, long discovery phases, and extra fees for files or usage rights.

## How to choose in 3 questions

1. **What's your budget?** Under $150: Fiverr. $250 to $2,000: independent designer. $5,000+: an agency becomes realistic.
2. **How long must the brand last?** If it's a test, go cheap. If you'll print packaging, wrap vehicles or trademark it, invest in an original identity.
3. **Do you need strategy or design?** If you already know your audience and positioning, you need a designer. If you don't, an agency's strategy work may be worth the price.

## Hidden costs to ask about

| Question | Why it matters |
| --- | --- |
| Are vector source files included? | Without AI/EPS/SVG files, every printer and developer will ask you again |
| Is copyright transferred in writing? | You need it to trademark the logo and stop copies |
| How many concepts and revision rounds? | "Unlimited revisions" on one idea is different from three real directions |
| Are fonts licensed for commercial use? | Some fonts need a paid licence for logos |
| Are guidelines included? | Without them, the brand drifts the first time someone else designs a flyer |

## FAQ

**Is Fiverr safe for a logo?**
Yes, if you choose a seller with a strong portfolio and reviews, and you get the source files and copyright in writing.

**Why do freelancers cost more than Fiverr gigs?**
Usually because they include research, more concepts, a full identity system and guidelines, not one logo file.
