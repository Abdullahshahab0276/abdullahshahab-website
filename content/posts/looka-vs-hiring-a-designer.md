**Short answer:** Looka is the cheaper choice if you need a placeholder logo today and may change it later. Hiring a designer is the better choice if the logo has to last, be trademarked, or work on packaging, signage and social media as one system. Looka's one-time logo packages cost $35 to $72 in 2026; a professional designer typically costs $250 to $2,000.

## What does Looka cost in 2026?

Looka sells one-time logo packages and yearly brand kit subscriptions. In September 2026 it raised its logo prices: the Basic Logo Package went from $20 to $35 and the Premium Logo Package from $65 to $72, while brand kit prices stayed the same ([PricingSaaS](https://pricingsaas.com/companies/looka/diffs/2026W38)). The Brand Kit Subscription is $96 a year and the Brand Kit Web Subscription $129 a year ([CostBench](https://costbench.com/changelog/looka-price-decrease-2026-02/)).

## What about Tailor Brands?

Tailor Brands started as an AI logo maker and has become a small-business bundle built around LLC formation, with branding tools inside ([Startup Stash](https://startupstash.com/alternatives/tailor-brands/)). Its plans are billed yearly: a free Lite plan that includes LLC formation and 8 free logos, and an Essential plan at $199 a year ([xpay](https://www.xpay.sh/agent-ready-index/tailor-brands/)). If you're forming a company anyway, the logo is a bonus. If you only want a logo, it's an indirect way to buy one.

## When Looka is enough

- You're testing an idea and may rename it in a few months.
- The logo will only appear on a simple website or invoice.
- Your budget for design is under $100 and you need something now.

In those cases, buy the cheapest package, keep the vector file, and plan a proper identity once the business works.

## When to hire a designer instead

- **You plan to trademark the name or logo.** Generic icon-and-wordmark combinations are often too close to existing marks. I explain the copyright side in [Can you copyright an AI-generated logo?](/blog/can-you-copyright-an-ai-generated-logo/)
- **You sell a physical product.** Packaging, labels and shelf presence need a system, not one file. See [packaging design](/packaging-design/).
- **You're in a crowded category** like beauty, coffee or fitness, where template logos look like everyone else's.
- **You'll spend money on ads, signage or printing.** A $35 logo on a $3,000 vehicle wrap is a false saving.

## Is Looka "AI-designed"?

Looka generates options from your inputs (name, industry, styles you like) by combining icons, fonts and layouts. That's why it's fast and cheap, and also why results in the same industry can look alike. A human designer starts from your business: who buys, where they see you, and what competitors already own. For a longer comparison, read [AI logo generator vs a human designer](/blog/ai-logo-generator-vs-human-designer/).

## FAQ

**Can I upgrade a Looka logo later?**
Yes. Many clients come to me with an AI logo and ask for a [rebrand](/rebranding/). We keep what customers already recognise, usually the name and colour, and redesign the mark properly.

**Do I own a Looka logo?**
Looka's paid packages give you the files and the right to use them, but icons and fonts may also be available to other users, so the design isn't exclusive. Read the current terms before you buy.

**Is Tailor Brands still a logo maker?**
It still makes logos, but its main plans are now LLC formation bundles. Compare it with [other ways to get a logo](/blog/99designs-alternatives/).

**What's the cheapest way to get a real, human-designed logo?**
A marketplace freelancer is cheapest, but quality varies a lot. A fixed-price independent designer like me costs more but includes research, concepts, guidelines and copyright.
