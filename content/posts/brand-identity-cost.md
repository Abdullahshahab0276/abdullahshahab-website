A complete brand identity costs anywhere from **about $300 to over $15,000 in the US, and roughly £1,500 to £20,000 in the UK**, depending on who designs it and how much strategy is included. Most small businesses and startups land between $500 and $5,000. The price depends less on the logo and more on everything around it: guidelines, applications, strategy and how many people work on it.

This guide covers brand identities, not just logos. For logo-only prices, read [how much does a logo cost in 2026](/blog/how-much-does-a-logo-cost/).

## What counts as a brand identity?

A brand identity is the full visual system: logo and its variations, colour palette, typography, imagery style, brand guidelines and the first set of applications such as stationery, social templates and packaging. The [brand identity checklist](/blog/brand-identity-checklist/) lists every deliverable to ask for.

## Why do brand identity prices vary so much?

- **Strategy.** Workshops, research and positioning documents add the most cost. A visual identity without them is far cheaper.
- **Team size.** An agency bills for strategists, account managers and several designers. One designer doesn't.
- **Number of applications.** Ten packaging designs cost more than one business card.
- **Location and overhead.** Office costs in London or New York are built into the price, not into the design.
- **Rounds and timeline.** Six weeks of meetings costs more than a focused 48-hour first draft.

## How much should a startup spend on its brand identity?

Match the budget to the stage of the business:

- **Pre-launch or testing an idea:** a logo with basic guidelines. Budget $250–$1,000.
- **Launching to customers:** a complete identity with three directions, guidelines and a social kit. Budget $450–$3,000.
- **Raising money or entering a crowded market:** add strategy, voice and marketing assets. Budget $1,000 to agency level.

Spending more doesn't guarantee a better result. Ask for the designer's own past work, the exact list of files, how many concepts and revisions you get, and who owns the copyright.

## The bottom line

In the US, expect $300 to $1,200 from a contest, $1,000 to $4,000 from an experienced freelancer, and $5,000 or more from an agency. In the UK, a starter identity is £1,500 to £5,000 and a full agency identity is £5,000 to £20,000. Decide what you need first (logo, system or strategy), then pay for that level and no more.
