Brand identity design

Brand identity design services for founders who want to look established

A brand identity is the whole visual system around your logo: colour, typography, imagery, guidelines and the assets you use every day. I design complete identities for startups and small businesses, from the first concept to the final files, with no agency layers in between.

What is included in a brand identity?

A complete identity gives your business one consistent look on your website, packaging, social media and ads. Depending on the package, you receive:

  • A logo system with concepts, variations and vector source files
  • A colour palette with codes for print and screen
  • Brand typography and imagery or graphic styles
  • Brand guidelines covering logo usage, colour, type and imagery
  • Stationery, 3D mockups and a social media kit (from Premium Branding)
  • Brand voice, marketing angles, ad creatives, packaging and UI assets (The Brand Ecosystem)

Use the brand identity checklist to see what to ask any designer for.

Who is this brand identity service for?

  • New businesses launching a product or service that needs to look credible from day one (see startup branding).
  • Growing businesses whose current look no longer matches the quality of what they sell (see rebranding).
  • Founders who want one designer from brief to final files instead of an agency team.

How is the identity designed?

Every project starts with research into your market and competitors. I then build the logo, choose the colours and typography, and test the system on real applications: business cards, packaging, signage, app screens and social posts. Your first full brand board arrives within 48 hours, and we refine it together until it feels right.

See finished identities in the Voitex, Ovenox, Mavion and Pingup case studies.

Brand identity design FAQs

What is the difference between a logo and a brand identity?

A logo is one mark. A brand identity is the full system around it: colours, typography, imagery, guidelines and the assets that keep your business consistent everywhere.

How long does a brand identity take?

The first full brand board arrives within 48 hours. Basic and Premium Branding are completed in 2 days, and The Brand Ecosystem in 5 business days.

Do I get brand guidelines?

Yes. Every package includes brand guidelines, and Premium Branding and The Brand Ecosystem include complete guidelines with logo usage, colour, type and imagery rules.

Do you work with clients outside Pakistan?

Yes. I work remotely with founders in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE, by call, email and WhatsApp.

Can I get a custom quote?

Yes. If your project doesn't fit a package, book a call or send a message and I'll put together a custom quote.
