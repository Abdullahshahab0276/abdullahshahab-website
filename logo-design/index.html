What do you get with a custom logo?

A logo is more than one file. Every package delivers a logo you can actually use everywhere your business shows up:

  • Logo concepts: 1 concept on the Basic plan, 3 distinct directions on Premium Branding and The Brand Ecosystem.
  • Vector source files plus transparent PNGs and print-ready PDFs (see which logo file format to use).
  • Colour palette and typography chosen to match the logo.
  • 3D mockups and stationery so you see the logo on real products before you print anything.
  • Brand guidelines explaining how to use the logo correctly.

Why choose a human logo designer over an AI logo maker?

AI logo makers are fast, but they pull from the same shapes and fonts as thousands of other businesses, and there is no one to explain the thinking. A human designer starts from your market, your competitors and your customers.

Ownership matters too. In the US, a purely AI-generated logo can't be registered for copyright, while a human-designed one can. I explain the details in can you copyright an AI-generated logo? Every logo I deliver is designed by me, so the copyright transfers to you with the files.

How does logo design in 48 hours work?

Basic and Premium packages are delivered in 2 days. The Brand Ecosystem, which adds strategy and marketing assets, takes 5 business days.

  • 1. Brief: a short call or brief about what you sell, who it's for and the look you want.
  • 2. Concepts in 48 hours: I research your industry and competitors, then deliver your first brand board with logo concepts, colours, type and mockups.
  • 3. Refine and finalise: revise until it feels right (2 revisions on Basic, 3 on Premium, unlimited on your chosen concept with The Brand Ecosystem), then receive every file.

What does a professional logo cost?

Logo design prices range from free AI tools to agency projects of $10,000 or more. My packages are fixed and paid once: $250 (Basic), $450 (Premium Branding) and $1,050 (The Brand Ecosystem). Logo design FAQs

How long does logo design take?

Your first brand board with logo concepts arrives within 48 hours of the brief. Basic and Premium packages are completed in 2 days; The Brand Ecosystem takes 5 business days.

How many logo concepts will I get?

The Basic plan includes 1 logo concept. Premium Branding and The Brand Ecosystem include 3 distinct concepts so you can compare directions.

Will I own the copyright to my logo?

Yes. You receive the source files and the copyright. Because the logo is designed by a person rather than generated by AI, it can be protected by copyright.

What file formats do I receive?

Vector source files plus transparent PNG and print-ready PDF files, so the logo works on the web, in print and on social media.

Do you use AI to design logos?

No. Every concept is researched, drawn and refined by me. That is also what makes the copyright yours.

Can you register my logo as a trademark?

I don't file trademarks, but I supply clean, original source files that you can take to a trademark attorney or filing service.
