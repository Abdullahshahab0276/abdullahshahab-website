Background

I hold a BS in Graphic Design (Fine Arts). My work started with logos and grew into full identity systems: guidelines, packaging, social media kits and ad creatives.

I'm based in Pakistan and work remotely with founders in the USA, UK, Canada, Australia and the UAE, by call, email and WhatsApp.

How I work

Every project starts with research into your market and competitors. Then I design, by hand, a logo system that works at favicon size and billboard size, and test it on the places your brand will actually live. Your first full brand board arrives within 48 hours.

I don't use AI to design logos. That is a quality decision and an ownership one: a human-designed logo can be protected by copyright, so the brand you receive is genuinely yours.

What I design

  • Logo design and logo systems
  • Brand identity design and brand guidelines
  • Startup branding and rebranding
  • Packaging, social media kits and ad creatives
